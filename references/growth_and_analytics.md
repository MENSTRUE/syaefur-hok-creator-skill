# DISTRIBUTION, MARKETING, CONTENT ANALYTICS

## Publish kit by platform

### YouTube long-form

- Strong searchable title ~45–65 characters if possible, 1 clear promise, relevant keyword "Honor of Kings" / "HOK".
- First 2 description lines explain problem and payoff. Add chapter timestamps only after verified timeline.
- Thumbnail 16:9 1280×720 recommended editorial canvas, 2–5 words and one focal point; don't assert a patch change without source.
- Pin comment with one question; add chapters and related Shorts when possible.

### YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels

- Use 9:16, e.g. 1080×1920; catchy spoken hook within 2 sec.
- Burn-in or platform captions (proofread Tegal). CTA after value.
- Make shareable standalone clip; avoid title/deadline that will become stale.
- Draft 2 titles, one caption each, 3–6 relevant hashtags, not generic hashtag stuffing.
- Verify platform duration and music rights when uploading. YouTube Shorts supports up to 3 min as of writing, but keep cuts sized to content.

## HOK Studio

Treat Studio campaign separately from normal social upload. Maintain two checkboxes: `(a) social published and URL captured`, `(b) eligible and submitted per active mission instructions`. No assumption that platform stats update instantly; don't infer reward eligibility from screenshot counters. Keep submission evidence link and date.

## KPI dashboard (do not invent numbers)

Long-form: impressions, CTR, 30s retention, avg view duration, percentage viewed, subs per 1000 views, comments & shares; Short: viewed vs swiped away (when available), avg percentage viewed, 3s hook, rewatch, likes per view, saves/shares, gained followers. Different platforms vary on metric names.

Interpret diagnostics conditionally:

| Observed pattern | Possible hypothesis | One test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low CTR but retention decent | title/thumbnail not promising enough | A/B two thumbnail angles |
| Big drop in first 2 seconds | slow opening or unclear result | cold open with result first |
| Good retention low follow | no clear reason to subscribe | consistent series branding + CTA |
| Views good, shares low | content entertaining but low utility | add concrete takeaway screenshot |
| Long retention dips mid-video | repetitive chapter | remove redundant lines / tighten transition |

Do not infer platform causality from one clip; require comparable sample size and timing, acknowledge small data. Never promise algorithm "hack" or guaranteed viral upload time.

## Simple weekly review output

1. Best/worst performance with actual numbers and denominators.
2. Top 2 hypotheses, one prioritized experiment.
3. Which topic/format to repeat versus retire.
4. Asset/SFX moments producing strong retention if observable, otherwise mark unknown.
5. Next 7 uploads with source and effort estimate.

## Optimization goals for a busy creator

Prioritize reuse templates, batch VO, recording library of common phrases, 3–7 short buffer, use existing maskot files, default motion presets, no need for CapCut Pro. Avoid overediting videos that won't get published. Track actual production time per content for effort forecasting.
